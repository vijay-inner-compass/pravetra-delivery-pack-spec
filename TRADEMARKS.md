# Trademark Guidelines

**Document Version**: `1.0.0`

---

## 1. Overview & Ownership

The names **"Pravetra"**, **"Pravetra Conductor"**, **"Yuktelos"**, and the associated stylized logos and branding marks are trademarks of **Yuktelos**.

Publication of the **Universal Delivery Pack Specification** under an open license grants rights to read, implement, copy, and distribute the specification prose and schemas, but **does not grant any rights to Yuktelos or Pravetra trademarks, trade dress, or logos**.

---

## 2. Permitted Compatibility Statements

Third-party software vendors, open-source projects, and autonomous agent authors are warmly welcomed to build implementations that produce or consume Delivery Packs.

You **MAY** use the trademarked name in nominative fair-use statements that accurately describe technical compatibility:

### Permitted Examples:
- *"Implements the Pravetra Universal Delivery Pack Specification v1.0"*
- *"Conformant with Pravetra Universal Delivery Pack Specification (Execution Profile)"*
- *"Compatible with Delivery Packs generated by Pravetra"*
- *"An open-source adapter for the Pravetra Delivery Pack protocol"*

---

## 3. Prohibited Usages

To avoid confusing developers or implying official endorsement where none exists:

### Prohibited Examples:
- You **MUST NOT** describe third-party products as *"Pravetra-certified"*, *"Official Pravetra Edition"*, or *"Pravetra Verified"* unless enrolled in a formal certification program established by Yuktelos.
- You **MUST NOT** use "Pravetra" or "Pravetra Conductor" as the primary name of your third-party software, product, or domain name (e.g., you cannot name a tool *"Pravetra Runner"* or *"Pravetra Desktop"*).
- You **MUST NOT** incorporate Yuktelos or Pravetra logos into your product branding, icons, or social media avatars without written authorization.
- You **MUST NOT** describe third-party tools as "open-source Pravetra Conductor". Conductor is a proprietary closed-source product.
